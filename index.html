Income & Education in Denmark

An interactive analysis of socioeconomic factors across Danish municipalities.

Introduction

Welcome to our final project for the DTU Social Data course. Here, we analyze the relationship between average income, education levels, and employment rates across different municipalities in Denmark using data from Statistics Denmark.

Interactive Map & Ternary Plot

How to use this tool: Click on any municipality on the map on the left. The map will highlight your selection, and the ternary plot on the right will isolate the exact socioeconomic breakdown (Employed, Unemployed, Enrolled) for that specific municipality.
